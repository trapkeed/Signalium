Signalium Platform Phase S15
Repository: signalium

Objective:

Transform Signalium into an enterprise-grade fintech platform with a futuristic technology startup design language.

The design philosophy is:

Premium.
Minimal.
Modern.
Professional.
Trustworthy.

Avoid:

* AI slop
* excessive animations
* crowded dashboards
* developer terminology
* technical research jargon
* cryptocurrency influencer aesthetics

The product should feel closer to:

institutional fintech software

than

retail AI trading bots.

---

## DESIGN LANGUAGE

Design style:

* futuristic
* premium
* enterprise
* minimalistic
* elegant
* high contrast
* mobile first
* responsive

Visual inspiration:

modern fintech startups,
premium SaaS products,
institutional trading software.

Avoid cyberpunk aesthetics.

Avoid neon overload.

Avoid hacker themes.

Use restrained futuristic design.

---

## COLOR SYSTEM

Primary colors:

deep navy
graphite
dark slate
white
silver accents

Accent colors:

gold
electric blue

Use accent colors sparingly.

Avoid rainbow dashboards.

---

## TYPOGRAPHY

Use modern professional typography.

Large spacing.

Readable hierarchy.

Strong contrast.

Comfortable mobile reading.

## LANDING PAGE

* professional market intelligence
