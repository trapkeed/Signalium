# SIGNALIUM S19 — INSTITUTIONAL TRADE TELEMETRY, POSITION INTELLIGENCE AND CONTINUOUS LEARNING LOOP

Repository:
signalium

Related Repository:
signalium-trainer

Objective:

Create the infrastructure that allows every decision, position, management action and outcome to become future learning material for the QDE.

The system must behave similarly to an experienced discretionary trader that remembers every trade it has ever taken.

The QDE must not simply remember wins and losses.

It must remember:

* why it entered
* why it exited
* what changed
* what it missed
* what it would do differently

---

---

---

17. ARCHITECTURAL RULE

---

Signalium stores telemetry.

Signalium Trainer learns from telemetry.

The QDE evolves through training.

No runtime heuristics may override the model.

No rule engine may become the decision maker.

The QDE remains:

the trader

the strategist

the risk manager

the portfolio manager

the execution manager

the sole decision maker.

Implement production-grade code only.

Avoid placeholders.

Maintain complete traceability and auditability.
